ScanSheet

Scan barcodes straight into Google Sheets

ScanSheet is an Android app by Livin Labs. Point your phone at a barcode or QR code and it adds a row to your spreadsheet: the code, the time, and any other columns you set up, such as quantity, location or notes.

What it does

  • Scans barcodes and QR codes with the phone camera.
  • Adds each scan as a new row in a Google Sheet you create or choose, or saves it on the phone as an Excel or CSV file.
  • Checks scans against a list, such as stock or attendees, and flags codes that are missing or repeated.
  • Lets several phones scan into the same Google Sheet together.

How ScanSheet uses your Google account

If you choose to connect Google, ScanSheet asks for two permissions:

  • Google Sheets: to create a spreadsheet for your scans and add a row for each scan.
  • Only the Google Drive files you use with ScanSheet: to list the spreadsheets you made or chose in the app, and to share one with teammates when you scan together.

ScanSheet can't see the rest of your Drive. It uses your Google data only to provide these features, and never sells it or uses it for ads. You can also use the app without Google by saving to the phone. Read the full Privacy Policy.
